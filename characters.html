Position

Height

Character Overview

Kei Tsukishima is a first-year student at Karasuno High School and a member of the volleyball team. He is known for his tall stature, sharp intellect, and analytical approach to the game. Despite his initially aloof demeanor, he gradually develops strong bonds with his teammates and becomes a key player in their matches.
